# KeepMind OS

Teams collaborate with people toward business outcomes, within explicitly chosen project boundaries.

## Language

**Project**:
An ongoing business objective with its own team, conversations, deliveries and permitted resources.
_Avoid_: Task label

**Company standard**:
A reusable set of company rules, voice, skills and connected accounts that projects may explicitly adopt. Company rules remain applicable when project-specific rules are added.
_Avoid_: Global permission

**Connection**:
A named authenticated identity for a service, such as a mailbox, WhatsApp number or Second Brain context. Sharing a service name does not mean sharing an account.
_Avoid_: Tool

**Delivery**:
A versioned result produced by a team within a conversation, which may be revised or approved by the user.
_Avoid_: Message
